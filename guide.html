Roomcode

Hear from every student in 10 minutes

A live Q&A and poll room for your class. Students join from their phones by scanning a code. No app, no accounts, nothing to install.

Run a 10-minute session

  1. Before
    Open the site and tap “Start a room”. Name it, e.g. “Period 3 · Unit review”.
  2. 0:00
    Show the code. Tap Projector view and put it on the board. It shows the QR, the top questions and live poll results.
  3. 0:01
    Say one line (below). Students scan, type a nickname and they're in.
  4. 0:02
    Let questions come in. Students upvote the ones they want answered. Answer the top ones out loud and tap Mark answered.
  5. 0:06
    Run a quick poll (2–4 answers): a check for understanding, an exit ticket, or “which topic next?”.
  6. 0:09
    Pin the takeaway: the next step or the homework, at the top of every phone.
  7. 0:10
    End the room, then Copy recap: every question with its votes, and the poll results, for your notes.

“Scan the code, pick a nickname. Ask anything you're stuck on. You can hide your name. Vote up the questions you want answered.”

Good uses

  • Review day: “What's still confusing?”
  • Exit ticket poll at the end of a lesson
  • Discussion warm-up or debate vote
  • Club meetings, assemblies, guest speakers

Privacy and safety

  • Students give a nickname only: no email, no sign-up, no ads.
  • “Hide my name” hides it from the room, so shy students ask too.
  • You moderate: pin, answer, close polls, end the room.
  • Rooms close after 24 hours.
  • If your school filters sites, open the link once before class to check that it loads.
